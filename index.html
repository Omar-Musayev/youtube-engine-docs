YouTube Engine

YouTube Engine is a privately operated desktop tool for producing educational videos and managing the operator’s own YouTube channel. The application runs locally and uses the YouTube Data, Analytics, and Reporting APIs.

What the application does

It prepares videos for review, checks the authorized channel, and retrieves channel and video performance reports. Its publishing tools support reviewed private uploads and require separate approval for public scheduling. Authorizing the app alone does not approve or schedule a video.
